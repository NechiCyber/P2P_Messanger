NECHI P2P

peer-to-peer secure chat · prototype

Not connected
Prototype, not audited security software. Messages use WebRTC's encrypted transport plus AES-GCM with a shared passphrase. Exchange the passphrase through a separate trusted channel. The manual offer/answer exchange is not automatically authenticated.

01 Identity & encryption

Enter the exact same secret on both devices. Do not send it in the offer/answer text. A longer random passphrase is safer.

02 Connect devices

Workflow: A creates an offer and sends it to B. B pastes it, reviews and accepts it, then sends the answer back to A. A pastes the answer and selects “Apply answer”. Use a trusted channel to exchange the shared secret separately.

03 Encrypted chat

Messages will appear here after the connection opens.

No chat history is saved by this page. Refreshing or closing the tab clears the visible conversation.
